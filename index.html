How to Play

1. Set Up

Name both teams, add your own questions, or load a preset pack.

2. Reveal Answers

Tap an answer tile to reveal it and add its points to the round total. Each answer can be revealed only once per round.

3. Strikes

Open Host Controls and tap Strike for a wrong answer. After three strikes, the other team gets a steal opportunity.

4. Award Points

Tap Award Points to add the round total to the active team's score. Switch Team to change which team is active.

5. Next Round

Tap Next Round to continue. After the final question, the winner screen displays the scores.

6. Save Your Questions

Use Export Questions to download a JSON file. Import Questions loads a previously saved pack.
